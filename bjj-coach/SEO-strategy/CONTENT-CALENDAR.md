## Editorial standards (applies to all phases)

### Word count floors
- Technique page: ≥400 words unique text (excluding schema, cards)
- Position page: ≥600 words
- Gym city page: ≥250 words (city context + listings count)
- Gym detail page: ≥150 words (small but verified data is fine here)
- Athlete page: ≥300 words
- Glossary term: ≥80 words
- Blog post: ≥800 words

### Required elements per page
- Unique meta title (≤60 chars) and meta description (≤155 chars)
- At least one original image OR one embedded video
- At least 5 internal links (in + out)
- Schema JSON-LD matching page type
- Named reviewer / author with linked `/team/{slug}`
- `datePublished` and `dateModified` visible and in schema
- Breadcrumb trail

### AI-assistance policy
- AI may draft. Humans must edit for accuracy, tone, and brand voice.
- Technique/injury content MUST be reviewed by a named black belt before publishing.
- Disclose AI assistance on `/editorial-policy`.
- Never publish AI-generated coach biographies, quotes, or lineage claims — these are fabrication risks.

### Freshness policy
- Reviewed every 12 months minimum
- Major techniques (top 100 by traffic) reviewed every 6 months
- Event/athlete pages updated after each major competition
- `dateModified` bumped only on substantive edits (not cosmetic)
