# Iteration 2 — Geo (Gyms, Cities, States)

**Ship this second, and only after Iteration 1 clears its exit criteria.** The geo pillar has the highest *commercial* value of any programmatic pillar (CPC: $1.20 for "bjj gyms near me"), but it's also the most data-intensive and the biggest operational liability for a solo operator. Treating it as an independent iteration lets you decide whether it's worth the work based on real Iteration 1 traction.

## Exit criteria from Iteration 1 (must-have before starting)

- ≥600 content pages live and indexed
- Indexation rate ≥85%
- ≥100 keywords ranking in top 10
- ≥3,000 monthly organic sessions
- No manual actions in GSC
- ≥3 months of clean random-sampling audits

If Iteration 1 is stalled or has quality issues, iteration 2 is a massive multiplier of those same issues at higher risk. Fix Iteration 1 first.

## What's in scope

| Page type | Volume target (end of Iteration 2) |
|---|---|
| State hub pages | 50 (all US states) |
| City pages | 25–50 (major US BJJ cities) |
| Gym detail pages | 300–500 (only verified) |

Deliberately modest volume. The gate on the gym pillar is **data quality**, not page count. A city page with 3 verified gyms beats a city page with 30 scraped-and-unverified gyms every single time — for user trust, for Google, and for legal/accuracy exposure.

## Why this is a separate iteration

1. **Data sourcing is the hard problem, not the templates.** See `DATA-SOURCING.md` — this is the document that will decide whether Iteration 2 is viable for you.
2. **Freshness requirements are ongoing.** Gym addresses, phone numbers, and schedules change. The content pillar can sit unchanged for a year and stay accurate; the geo pillar needs re-verification roughly annually.
3. **Thin-content risk is acute.** A city page with weak data is a textbook thin page. Either ship it rich or don't ship it.
4. **Commercial intent enables a monetization path.** Featured gym listings ($29–99/mo) become a real revenue line if the directory gets traction. The content pillar is "free forever"; the geo pillar is "free now, paid listings later."
